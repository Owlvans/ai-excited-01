A giant full moon sits directly behind its head like a halo. Its two eyes are blazing white-gold suns with starburst pupils, round and wide open in amazement, rays of light shooting outward — the brightest thing in the image. Far below, a tiny human silhouette on a cliff holds up a small lantern, the only witness. Deep midnight blue and violet, gold and white highlights, one clear focal point: the eyes. main.py copies the committed entry.png to the output directory, byte-identical on every run, no network.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes entry.png to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR, or /output when that is unset.
